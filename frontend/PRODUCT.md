# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TopicEye serves content creators who need a high-signal view of emerging topics, plus administrators who operate the source, scoring, model-routing, and reporting systems behind that workflow. The AI engine workspace is used by operators who compare models, control routing, diagnose configuration, and balance output quality against latency and cost.

## Product Purpose

TopicEye turns multi-source content streams into explainable topic recommendations. Its administrative surfaces exist to keep that pipeline observable and controllable: operators should be able to understand the current state, make a change with confidence, verify it, and recover from failures without leaving the workflow.

## Brand Personality

Signal-first, transparent, pragmatic. The product should feel technically credible and calm under operational pressure, while retaining TopicEye's direct creator-focused voice.

## Anti-references

- Opaque AI dashboards that present recommendations or scores without showing their inputs, status, or routing context.
- Decorative SaaS analytics pages that prioritize large vanity metrics over actionable controls and diagnostics.
- Dense infrastructure consoles that expose every parameter at once and make routine tasks depend on expert knowledge.
- Generic RSS-reader interfaces that treat all incoming information as equally important.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next operational decision close to the evidence needed to make it.
2. Make model availability, routing order, cost, and test status legible at a glance.
3. Keep routine workflows concise; reveal expert configuration progressively.
4. Explain failures in context and provide a clear recovery action.
5. Preserve consistent admin-shell patterns so operators can transfer knowledge across pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation, and semantic status messaging. Respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid relying on color alone for state, and keep dense operational layouts usable at narrow desktop and mobile widths.
